      COMMUNITY WAR MACHINES
        1 / 7
        NOT A PUZZLE GAME

        Build an arsenal by day. Survive the war machine by night.

        Day: 14 moves to match munitions
        →
        They arm your turrets
        →
        Night: the battle

        Matching isn't the goal: it's how you build weapons. When your moves run out, a war machine attacks your base and everything you built fires back.

        EVERY MATCH ARMS A WEAPON

        Five munitions, five jobs

          Stack one kind to upgrade it: 5 shots reaches tier 2, 10 reaches tier 3 (M2 MG → Gatling → CIWS). Chains multiply what you collect.

          COMBOS CALL IN THE AIR FORCE

          Bigger matches, bigger weapons

          • MATCH 4Jet strike: a fighter strafes the whole row or column.
          • L or TGrenade: clears the 3×3 around it.
          • MATCH 5Airstrike beacon: swap it with any munition and a bomber hits every one of that kind. Also earns an airstrike.
          • CHAIN 4+Falling munitions keep matching: +1 airstrike. You can hold 9.
          NIGHT: THE BATTLE

          Your arsenal fires on its own. You call the airstrikes.

          • SHIELDArmour you matched goes up at nightfall and stays until it's knocked down.
          • REPAIRWrenches repair the base when the battle starts. Base already full? The repairs go into your shield.
          • AIRSTRIKETap CALL AIRSTRIKE during the battle. Don't save them until it's too late.
          • BASEYour base only falls in a night battle. Watch the bar under the turrets.

          Your whole arsenal fires first: the war machine only shoots back once your last shot has landed. Destroy it: next stage, base repaired. If it survives, it fires its attacks, unleashes its signature weapon, then holds the sky and shells you every 4 moves until you finish it.

          THE WAR MACHINES THINK WITH REAL CHIPS

          Every boss brain is a circuit

          Each war machine decides every attack with a NAND/LATCH chip (a TapeOut circuit on X Layer): Barrage, Focus Fire, EMP or Field Repair. LATCHes give it memory, so each one has a personality. Tap a boss's health bar to watch its brain think.

          WAYS TO PLAY

          Pick your war

          • SOLOHold the line as long as you can. Eight war machines, then they come back stronger.
          • VS AISame battlefield as an AI commander, live beside you. Hard brings veteran rules.
          • DAILY OPOne battlefield for everyone, every day.
          • RAIDOMEGA, the weekly world boss. Everyone's damage comes off one health pool.
          • FORGEDesign your own war machine's brain and let others fight it.
          • DUELSSend a friend your exact battlefield; your run plays beside them as a ghost.
